# Code of Conduct

We want Barepane to be a friendly, useful place for everyone who uses or contributes to it.

## Our standard

- Be respectful and patient. Assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Keep discussion on topic: the app, its bugs, and its direction.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Do not publish other people's private information (including server addresses, keys, or logs that contain them).

## Scope

This applies to the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and any space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block people who break these rules. To report a problem, email **usmanbhatti.dev@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
